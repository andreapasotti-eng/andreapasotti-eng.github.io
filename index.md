I'm a biomedical engineer based in Faenza, Italy. For over four years I have been developing and validating algorithms for biomedical signals and images, in clinical research (IRCCS Istituto delle Scienze Neurologiche di Bologna, University of Bologna) and in medical device R&D under ISO 13485, ISO 14971 and the MDR. I build my pipelines in MATLAB and Python, from preprocessing to quantitative analysis, and validate them against clinical references on real patient data.

### Current focus

The X-ray imaging chain: from raw detector data to calibrated images, image-quality metrics and cone-beam CT reconstruction.

### Experience

**Research fellow – Algorithms for biomedical signals** · University of Bologna, DEI · 2025–present
Algorithms for quantitative monitoring of motor development in preterm infants from wearable IMU signals. Automated segmentation of gait and U-turns, now applied to the group's whole database of thousands of recordings.

**R&D engineer, medical devices, and quality specialist** · DEAS S.r.l. · 2024–2025
Design of a vibrating-mesh nebulizer, from selection and characterisation of the piezoelectric actuator to verification. Risk analysis (ISO 14971) and verification protocols for disposables and PEEP valves.

**Biomedical engineer – Clinical movement analysis** · IRCCS Istituto delle Scienze Neurologiche di Bologna · 2022–2025
Designed and built the analysis pipeline of the ARCHIMEDE study on my own: 84 patients with three inertial sensors, assessed before spinal decompression surgery and at 30 and 90 days after it.

**MSc thesis – Medical image analysis** · Politecnico di Torino · 2022
Automatic segmentation of T1 brain MRI in 98 paediatric patients with Chiari malformation: wavelet and bilateral filtering, Demons atlas registration and GVF active contours. Dice 0.83 (brain), 0.73 (cerebellum) and 0.75 (posterior fossa) against manual segmentations, and 93.7% accuracy of an SVM predicting surgical indication on a held-out test set.
